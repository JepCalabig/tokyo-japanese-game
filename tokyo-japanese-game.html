Particles Reference

Core particles for polite Tokyo Japanese.

wa

Topic marker — “as for…”, “speaking of…”
Marks what the sentence is about. Does not always equal the grammatical subject.
watashi wa kankoukyaku desu
As for me, I am a tourist.

ga

Subject / focus marker
Highlights the subject, especially with new information, likes, abilities, or existence.
neko ga suki desu
I like cats.

o

Direct object marker
Marks the thing the action is done to.
ramen o kudasai
Ramen, please.

ni

Destination, time, indirect object, existence location
To / at a point / for.
gakkou ni ikimasu
I go to school.

de

Location of action or means / method
Where an action happens, or “by means of”.
kissaten de tabemasu
I eat at a café.

no

Possession or modifier (“of”, “’s”)
Connects two nouns.
watashi no hon
My book.

to

“And” (complete list) or “with”
ringo to banana
Apples and bananas.

mo

“Also”, “too”, “even”
watashi mo ikimasu
I will go too.

kara

“From”
koko kara
From here.

made

“Until”, “up to”, “as far as”
go-ji made
Until 5 o’clock.

e

Direction (“toward”, “to”)
shibuya e ikimasu
I go toward Shibuya.

ka

Question marker
Turns a statement into a yes/no question.
gakusei desu ka?
Are you a student?

ni vs de

ni = destination or point.
de = place of action or means.

wa vs ga

wa = known topic.
ga = focus or new information.
